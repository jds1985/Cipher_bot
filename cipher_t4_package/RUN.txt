3. Build the mix from the parent raw jsonl directory. CNN and synthetic files are skipped.
   python3 build_balanced.py --sources /path/to/by_source --out out
   Read out/quality.json and out/exclusions.jsonl. The fixture self-test does not certify this directory.
   SQuAD unanswerable templates are capped at 4 copies and at 2% of that source before the split, then pinned to train. No new phrasings are added.
   A short fact (at most 3 tokens and 32 characters, not a refusal) may recur and may appear in both splits, at most 8 times. Every such string is listed under assistant_audit.short_answer_exceptions.
   Any longer repeated answer keeps one copy. Val rows that still share one are dropped and logged.
   Lexical exclusions use the same predicate as the gate. Each record has the source file, source line, frozen id, reason, and similarity. It does not contain the held-out prompt.
   Licenses stay per source. Revisions are UNKNOWN.
   CC BY-SA rows do not clear trained weights for distribution.

4. Pack, tokenizer is outside this zip. Do this only after the real quality.json passes.
   python3 pack_rows.py --data out --tokenizer /path/to/tokenizer.json
   Read out/pack_report.json. A prompt that does not fit the model context of 1024 is left out of the bin. An answer that does not fit is truncated from the end, the end-of-turn token is kept when a slot remains, and the dropped token count is recorded. Generation context 512 is a separate condition from this pack.
